We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and secure your information when you use SABTRACK AI.

Information We Collect

SABTRACK AI processes and secures the following types of data to deliver personalized fitness and nutrition tracking:

  • Account Data: Hashed email addresses and password credentials. These are securely routed and managed via our database hosting providers (Supabase Authentication).
  • Nutritional Logs: Details of meals tracked, description queries, portion sizes, calorie counts, and macro profiles.
  • Fasting & Weight History: Personal progress statistics, fasting duration logs, and weight history to calculate health charts and personal goal progress.
  • Uploaded Images: Meal and food pictures captured by you to perform AI-based nutrition estimation. These images are transmitted directly to Google Gemini APIs to parse nutritional components.

How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is used strictly to provide the core services of the app, including:

  • Rendering personal fitness dashboards, progress statistics, and analytics.
  • Calculating active calorie requirements and adjusting nutritional milestones.
  • Synchronizing logs in real-time across your authorized devices.
  • Providing shared space coordination for community groups and friend connections.

Third-Party Services & API Integration

We do not sell, rent, or distribute your personal details to third-party advertising services. In order to provide key services, we partner with industry-standard secure services:

  • Supabase: For cloud data hosting, database integrity, and authentication APIs.
  • Google Gemini API: For processing meal image files and generating macronutrient breakdown values. Image files are not retained or saved by our server after analysis.

Data Retention & Deletion Rights

We honor your full rights over your data. At any time, you can wipe all your information from our database.

  • Immediate In-App Deletion: Clicking "Delete Account" inside the app settings instantly and permanently purges your user profile, nutrition logs, fasting logs, and weight metrics.
  • Web Requests: You can submit a manual deletion request online. Web requests are fully processed within 14 to 30 calendar days.
  • Compliance & Exception Logs: Minimal, anonymized request records may be kept temporarily solely for verification, security auditing, and fraud prevention purposes.
