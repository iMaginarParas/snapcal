Account & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your personal data. Below are instructions to delete your SABTRACK AI account in-app or request deletion online.

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the SABTRACK AI app installed on your device

1
Open App
Launch SABTRACK AI on your Android device
2
Profile Settings
Tap Profile tab > Settings icon (top right)
3
Account & Privacy
Select Account & Legal Settings
4
Delete Account
Tap Delete Account & confirm. Wipes data instantly.

Option 2: Submit Web Account Deletion Request

Use this form if you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in.

✅ Your deletion request has been submitted successfully! We will process your account deletion within 14 to 30 days and send a confirmation to your email.
⚠️ Please enter a valid registered email address and check the confirmation box.

Data Policy & Retention Details

As required by Google Play Data Safety Guidelines

Permanently Deleted Data
  • User Profile: Name, registered email address, hashed password, and avatar.
  • Fitness Logs: Step history, workout sessions, exercise reps/sets, and active calories.
  • Nutrition Data: Logged meals, macro targets, food images, and water intake history.
  • Progress Metrics: Weight logs, body measurements, fasting history, and badges.
  • Social Data: Group memberships, friend connections, and referral links.
Data Retention Period & Exceptions
  • Execution Timeframe: Deletion requests via web form are fulfilled within 14 to 30 calendar days.
  • Immediate In-App Purge: In-app deletion purges database records immediately.
  • System Security Logs: Anonymized server security logs may be retained for up to 30 days solely for security & fraud prevention compliance, after which they are permanently purged.
